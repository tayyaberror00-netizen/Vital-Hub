Knowledge Base

Patient FAQ

Common questions about the Vital Hub ecosystem.

Products
Are the medical devices FDA approved?

Yes, all electronic devices sold on Vital Hub are medically certified and undergo rigorous quality checks to meet international FDA and ISO standards. Each product page lists the applicable certifications. We also source exclusively from verified manufacturers with established clinical track records.

Products
Do the wearables work with both Android and iOS?

Yes. All Vital Hub wearables — including the TK9 Smart Ring and LIGE Fitness Band — are compatible with Android 6.0+ and iOS 11.0+. Simply download the Vital Hub app (available on both stores) and pair via Bluetooth. Step-by-step setup guides are included in every package and available digitally on your product page.

Products
What is the warranty period for devices?

All Vital Hub products carry a minimum 12-month manufacturer warranty. Premium items (smart rings, BP monitors, hearing amplifiers) come with an extended 24-month warranty. Warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. Physical damage and water damage are excluded unless the product is rated waterproof.

Products
Can I view products in 3D / AR before buying?

Absolutely. Products tagged "3D • AR" in the store support augmented reality previews on mobile devices. Open the store on your smartphone, tap "View in Room", and point your camera at a flat surface. This uses WebXR technology — no app download required for supported browsers (Chrome 81+, Safari 14+). AR capability is indicated on each product card.

AI
How does the X-Ray AI diagnostic work?

Our Neural X-Ray engine uses a convolutional neural network trained on over 200,000 annotated radiological images to detect skeletal anomalies, fractures, and alignment deviations with 99.8% accuracy. Simply upload your X-ray file (JPEG, PNG, or DICOM), and results are returned within seconds. Important: This is a preliminary screening tool and must be confirmed by a licensed radiologist or clinician before any medical decision is made.

AI
Is my health data private and secure?

Yes. All data transmitted through Vital Hub is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We are HIPAA-compliant and GDPR-aligned. Your health records, scans, and biometric data are never sold to third parties. You retain full ownership and can request complete deletion of your data at any time from your account settings.

AI
How accurate is the Nutrition AI meal planner?

The Nutrition AI uses your BMI, TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure), health goals, medical flags, and even your geographic location to generate region-specific 7-day meal plans. It factors in macronutrient ratios validated against WHO dietary guidelines. The plan is updated each time you submit new biometric data, ensuring it always reflects your current health status.

AI
Can the AI assistant replace a real doctor?

No — and we're transparent about this. Vital Hub's AI tools are designed to empower you with data-driven insights and support informed conversations with your doctor, not to replace clinical judgment. For any symptoms, diagnoses, or treatment decisions, always consult a licensed healthcare professional. Our Tele-Visit feature makes booking a real specialist fast and convenient.

Orders
How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Pakistan takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 business days) is available for major cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi. International shipping is available to 40+ countries with estimated delivery of 7–14 business days. All orders include a tracking number sent to your registered email within 24 hours of dispatch.

Orders
What is the return and refund policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on all unopened products. For defective items, we provide a free replacement or full refund within 60 days of purchase regardless of packaging. To initiate a return, log in to your account, navigate to Order History, and select "Request Return". Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Orders
Which payment methods are accepted?

Vital Hub accepts Visa, Mastercard, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and bank transfers (HBL, UBL, MCB). Cash on Delivery (COD) is available in selected cities. For international customers, we accept PayPal and major international credit cards. All transactions are SSL-secured and processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways.

Account
How do I book a tele-consultation?

Creating an account is free and takes under 2 minutes. Visit the Appointments page, choose your preferred specialist and time slot, then confirm with your registered email. You'll receive a secure video call link 15 minutes before your session. Consultations are available 24/7 across all time zones, with same-day slots often available for urgent queries.

Account
Can I manage multiple family members under one account?

Yes. Vital Hub supports Family Profiles — you can add up to 5 family members under a single account, each with their own biometric data, health history, and nutrition plans. Toggle between profiles from the account dashboard. This feature is ideal for managing elderly parents or children's health data alongside your own.

Account
Is there a subscription or membership fee?

Vital Hub is free to use for store browsing, AI chat, and basic nutrition plans. The Vital Pro membership (Rs 999/month) unlocks unlimited AI diagnostics, priority specialist bookings, advanced nutrition tracking, and family profiles. A 14-day free trial is available for new accounts — no credit card required to start.
